NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions before planning your Bali trip.

How can I book a tour?

Fill in the booking enquiry form or contact us through WhatsApp. Tell us your preferred tour, travel date and number of guests.

Are the tour packages private?

Our tour packages are offered as private tour options. Contact us to confirm the arrangements, vehicle options and details for your group.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Yes. You can discuss your preferred destinations and travel schedule with our team. We will help check the possible arrangements for your trip.

How much does a Bali tour cost?

Prices depend on your destinations, travel date, group size and service requirements. Contact us for a quotation before confirming your booking.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Airport pickup and drop-off can be requested through our transportation service. Please share your flight details, arrival time and destination so we can check availability.

Are entrance tickets included in the tour price?

Inclusions may vary depending on the package and quotation. Please confirm whether entrance tickets, parking, meals and activity fees are included before booking.

Can I book transportation only?

Yes. You can enquire about transportation without booking a tour package. Let us know your pickup location, destination, date and passenger requirements.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your booking is confirmed only after you receive confirmation from our team and complete any required booking arrangements.
